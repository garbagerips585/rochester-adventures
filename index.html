Start here

The leaves in the hills south of Rochester are expected to hit peak exactly this weekend. The plan: a city day Saturday while it is cold, the Canandaigua Lake wine loop Sunday, and Letchworth, the Grand Canyon of the East, on Monday when it is free, empty and the warmest day. Everything is a suggestion; the Epic and Rainy day sections have the swaps.

Monday

Letchworth: a 17-mile gorge with 600-foot cliffs and three big waterfalls, 50 minutes from the house and at peak. Monday is the day because the $10 gate fee ends Sunday, the weekend crowds that close the gates are gone, and it is the warmest day. If you fly out Monday we do the park in the morning and skip the rest.

Niagara Falls

1 hr 25 on the Thruway, or 1 hr 50 the scenic way

Take Route 104 west through the apple country to Lewiston, then the Niagara Scenic Parkway along the gorge (stop at Devil's Hole and Whirlpool State Parks for the rapids and the color). Niagara Falls State Park is free; Goat Island parking about $15. Maid of the Mist runs through Nov 8, $30.25, ponchos included and worth it in the cold. Cave of the Winds is open Sat and Sun ($23) but closed Mon Oct 19 and 20. Passports needed for the Canadian side.

Rochester in one minute

Rochester grew up on the Genesee River waterfalls: flour mills first (the Flour City), then nurseries and lilacs (the Flower City), then Kodak, Xerox and Bausch & Lomb (the World's Image Centre). George Eastman invented roll film here and gave the city its music school, museum and parks. Susan B. Anthony and Frederick Douglass lived and are buried here. The Erie Canal made it America's first boomtown in 1825. Wegmans started here in 1916. And yes, the garbage plate is a real thing, born at Nick Tahou's in 1918.

A few heads-ups

The Irondequoit Bay outlet bridge is swung open for boats until Nov 1, so no shortcut to Webster. Seabreeze and the beach carousel are closed for the season. Letchworth's weekend gates close when the lots fill, which is why we go Monday. Niagara's Cave of the Winds is closed Oct 19 and 20. Abbott's on the lake is at the end of its season; call first.
